## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Everything you need, all in one place.

Anston is one marketplace for almost anything: cars, homes and apartments, clothes, jewelry, homemade food, electronics and local services. Buy it now, bid on it, rent it, or book it, all from one app.

BUY AND BID
• Shop items at a set price, or make the seller an offer
• Bid on auctions and get notified when you're outbid
• Pay securely by card through Stripe

SELL IN MINUTES
• List an item, a rental or a service with photos in a few taps
• Sell at a fixed price, run an auction, or accept offers
• Buy shipping labels in the app, or offer local pickup
• Get paid straight to your bank account

RENT AND BOOK
• Rent cars, homes, apartments, tools and more by the hour, day, week or month
• Book local services like cleaning, landscaping, tutoring, childcare, music lessons and sports classes
• Pick open dates and times right from the seller's calendar

SAFE AND SIMPLE
• Message buyers and sellers inside the app
• Read reviews from real buyers before you buy
• Verified business pages for local shops and pros
• Report anything that breaks the rules, and our team reviews it

FOR EVERYONE
Anston is available in 21 languages, including English, Arabic, Spanish, Chinese, French, Urdu and Hindi.

Questions? Email support@myanston.com or visit myanston.com.
```

## App content answers (Policy → App content)

- **Privacy policy:** https://myanston.com/privacy
- **Ads:** No, the app has no ads.
- **App access:** Some features need an account. Give the reviewers a test login (email + password) with a listing on it.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over (Anston requires members to be 18+).
- **Content rating questionnaire:** Category "Shopping / marketplace". Users can talk to each other (messages) and buy physical goods, so say Yes to "users can interact" and "digital purchases: No".
- **Data safety:**
  - Collected: name, email address, phone number (if given), user IDs, photos (listings and profile), messages, purchase history, approximate location (city you pick).
  - Payment card details go straight to Stripe; Anston doesn't store them.
  - Data is encrypted in transit: **Yes**.
  - People can delete their account and data: **Yes**, in the app (Account → Delete account) or at https://myanston.com/account/delete.
  - Shared with third parties for ads: **No**.
- **Financial features:** No (Anston isn't a bank or lender; card payments are processed by Stripe).
- **Government app / news app / health app:** No.
